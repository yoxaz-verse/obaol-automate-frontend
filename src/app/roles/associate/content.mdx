# Associates — Registered Businesses on OBAOL

Associates are **registered companies and business entities** across the complete commodity trade ecosystem. They trade, source, move, verify, finance, and enable commodities on OBAOL Supreme. Individuals participate through the Operator role.

## The Trade Ecosystem

- **Commodity trade** — buying, selling, importing, exporting, and sourcing
- **Logistics and movement** — storage, packaging, transport, freight, and handoffs
- **Quality and compliance** — testing, inspection, customs, and documentation
- **Finance and technology** — funding, risk protection, procurement, and trade enablement

## Associate Business Directory

- **Traders**
- **Importers**
- **Exporters**
- **Warehouse Owners**
- **Inland Transportation**
- **Freight Forwarders**
- **Logistics Providers**
- **Suppliers**
- **Packaging Companies**
- **Quality Testing Labs**
- **Agritech Companies**
- **Customs Clearance Agencies**
- **Finance & Insurance Partners**
- **Procurement Partners**

## What Associate Companies Do
- Manage enquiries and execution milestones
- Coordinate logistics and movement across partners
- Align documentation and compliance workflows
- Coordinate with the other verified companies needed for execution

Each business role has a dedicated page explaining:
- Who can join
- What role they play in execution
- Its responsibilities and OBAOL workflow
- What the company must prepare before registration
