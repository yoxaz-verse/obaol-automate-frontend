# Associates — Registered Businesses on OBAOL

Associates are **registered companies and business entities** that buy commodities, sell commodities, do both, or provide verified trade services on OBAOL Supreme. Individuals participate through the Operator role.

## How Companies Participate

- **Buy commodities**
- **Sell commodities**
- **Buy and sell commodities**
- **Provide trade services**

## Associate Business Directory

- **Traders**
- **Importers**
- **Exporters**
- **Warehouse Owners**
- **Inland Transportation**
- **Freight Forwarders**
- **Logistics Providers**
- **Suppliers**
- **Packaging Companies**
- **Quality Testing Labs**
- **Agritech Companies**
- **Customs Clearance Agencies**
- **Finance & Insurance Partners**
- **Procurement Partners**

## What Associate Companies Do
- Manage enquiries and execution milestones
- Coordinate logistics and movement across partners
- Align documentation and compliance workflows
- Coordinate with the other verified companies needed for execution

Each business role has a dedicated page explaining:
- Who can join
- What role they play in execution
- Its responsibilities and OBAOL workflow
- What the company must prepare before registration
